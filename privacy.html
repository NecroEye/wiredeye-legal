Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WiredEye (“the App”) handles information. The App is developed and provided by Muratcan Gözüm (“I”, “me”).

Summary

  • The App primarily processes monitoring data on your device.
  • By default, we do not sell your data and do not share your monitoring logs with third parties.
  • If you contact support by email, we receive the email content and address for support purposes.

Information the App may process (on-device)

Depending on features you use, the App may process and display monitoring-related information locally, such as:

  • App/package identifiers related to network usage (e.g., which app generated traffic)
  • Network endpoints and metadata (e.g., IP/host, ports, protocol, timestamps)
  • Traffic volume statistics (bytes, throughput, packet rate)
  • Filters/search terms you enter inside the App

Note: The App is designed as a monitoring tool. It is your responsibility to use it only on devices and networks you are authorized to monitor.

Information we collect

Support emails. If you choose to contact support, we receive:

  • Your email address
  • The content of your email and any attachments you send

Information sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share your monitoring logs with third parties by default. We may share information only when required to comply with applicable laws or enforce our rights.

Third-party services

Currently, the App does not require third-party analytics/ads to function. If we add optional third-party services in the future (e.g., crash reporting, analytics), we will update this policy and, where required, request your consent.

Data retention

  • Monitoring data displayed by the App is stored locally on your device (if stored at all) and can be cleared from within the App.
  • Support emails are retained as needed to respond and provide support, then deleted when no longer necessary.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we handle. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this URL and will include a new effective date.
