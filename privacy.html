Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WiredEye (“the App”) handles information. The App is developed and provided by Muratcan Gözüm (“I”, “me”, “my”).

Summary

  • The App primarily processes monitoring data on your device.
  • By default, I do not sell your data and do not share your monitoring logs with third parties for advertising.
  • If you contact support by email, I receive your email address, message content, and any attachments you send.
  • Some core monitoring features may require app/package visibility and Android platform features such as VpnService and, where available, profile-related interoperability.

Information the App may process on-device

Depending on the features you use, the App may process and display monitoring-related information locally on your device, including:

  • App/package identifiers related to network usage
  • Installed app visibility information needed to map observed traffic to apps on the device
  • Network endpoints and metadata, such as IP/host, ports, protocol, and timestamps
  • Traffic volume statistics, such as bytes, throughput, and packet rate
  • Filters, searches, and view preferences you enter inside the App

How platform permissions and Android features may be used

App/package visibility

To provide app-level monitoring context, WiredEye may inspect package visibility on your device to determine which installed apps correspond to observed network activity. This information is used to power user-facing monitoring features inside the App, such as showing app-related traffic context, and is not used for advertising.

VPN-based monitoring

Certain monitoring features may use Android’s VpnService to create a local monitoring tunnel on the device so the App can inspect and present network metadata for the features you enable. This functionality is used for network monitoring and analysis within the App.

Work profile / cross-profile support

On supported devices, and only where Android, device configuration, and any applicable administrator policies allow it, WiredEye may support limited profile-related interoperability (for example, personal profile and work profile scenarios) to improve app context and monitoring visibility across profiles. Availability of these features depends on device support, profile configuration, user action, and administrator or OEM restrictions.
